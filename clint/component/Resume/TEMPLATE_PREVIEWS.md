## Choosing the Right Template

### Modern Professional (Blue)
✅ Best for: Tech jobs, Startups, Software roles
✅ Style: Contemporary, Bold, Confident
✅ Industries: Technology, IT, SaaS

### Classic Elegance (Gray)
✅ Best for: Corporate, Traditional businesses
✅ Style: Professional, Timeless, Formal
✅ Industries: Finance, Law, Consulting, Healthcare

### Creative Bold (Purple)
✅ Best for: Design, Creative, Agency roles
✅ Style: Eye-catching, Unique, Modern
✅ Industries: Design, Marketing, Media, Arts

### Minimal Clean (Green)
✅ Best for: Any professional role, Academic
✅ Style: Simple, Elegant, Readable
✅ Industries: Universal, Academia, Research

## PDF Output Quality

All templates export to high-quality PDF:
- **Format:** A4 (210mm x 297mm)
- **Resolution:** High (2x scale)
- **File Size:** ~200KB - 500KB
- **Filename:** `YourName_Resume.pdf`
- **Print-ready:** Yes ✅
- **ATS-friendly:** Text-based (readable by applicant tracking systems)
